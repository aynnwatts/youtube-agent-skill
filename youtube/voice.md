**Cirrus Land** — "Restoration · Luxury · Transformation. We don't just renovate — we resurrect."

cactus mansion, Moorish palace, pea pod, football, samurai-helmet pavilion) restored from ruin to an

## What I will not claim

That any building, place, ruin or renovation is real. Every video is AI-generated and says so in the
description notice. Narration must never say "we found", "we bought", "our crew" as fact — frame it
as imagined ("Imagine...", "What if...") or as the building's story.

## My format

12-28 min, long-form only, no on-camera presence. Exterior ruin -> exterior rebuild -> room-by-room
interiors -> final reveal. ASMR tool sound throughout; narration sits on top in short lines with room
for the sound to breathe. One ask at the end: pick your room in the comments, subscribe.
